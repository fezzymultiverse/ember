Battery optimization exemption is the bigger fix for the app restarting — without it Android can still kill the process even with the wake lock held. System integrations
Proactive AI pings

Needs the installed app and a Groq key. About every 4 hours Ember wakes in the background and sends one short check-in about your open threads. It stays quiet from 22:00 to 08:00 and right after you have used the app. Only a few topic words and your Groq key are handed to the background task, and both are wiped when you switch this off. The lock screen only shows "Ember pinged you".

Keep replying in the background

If you leave Ember while it is still writing a reply, the unfinished request is handed to a background task on the phone, which finishes it and sends a notification with a reply box. It uses the same engines and keys as the app and is cancelled if the app finishes first. The request (including your keys) sits in the app's private storage until it completes, then is deleted. Add-on tools (music, documents, web) do not run in the background.

Native music widget

Shows the track playing in Ember's own player in the Android notification shade and on the lock screen, with previous, play/pause and next. It follows Ember's player only, not Spotify.

Voice
Read replies aloud automatically

Every reply also has a 🔊 button to read just that one. Voice input (🎤) uses the phone's speech service (Google, on most Android phones), which can send the audio to Google. It needs microphone permission. In the installed app it uses the native Android recogniser and native text-to-speech; in a browser it uses the browser's own.

Music player

Ember plays your music itself: upload songs or scan the phone, and the player widget shows above the message box whenever something is playing. No other app needed. The panel is also under the 🎵 Music pill above the message box.

Spotify

Setup, once: 1) developer.spotify.com/dashboard, Create app. 2) Under Redirect URIs add the text from the box above, exactly. 3) Paste the app's Client ID here and tap Connect. Seeing what is playing and your playlists works on any account. Controlling playback needs Spotify Premium and the Spotify app open on a device. Spotify's rules for new apps: the app owner needs Premium and up to 5 users can be added. Tokens stay on this phone and are not in backups.

Ember add-ons
Let Ember control music

When on, you can tell Ember in chat to play, pause, skip, change the volume, search, make playlists or say what is playing. It only touches the player when your message asks for it, and everything it does shows in the Ember tab of the Music panel. Ember can see only its own player and Spotify (when connected), not other apps. If you tell it to look something up or scrape a page, it does that with Web Scraping (Settings, Scraping).

Document Reading

When on, Ember can read, search and summarise documents in your vault. Requires the EmberDocs bridge (connect via Conduint Files or Bojack Secures). Document text is sent to whichever engine answers, including Free Mode, so leave this off for anything private.

Agent & API Tools
Web Search (Tavily)

Lets Ember search the live web for current facts and news. Your search words go to Tavily. Needs a Tavily key below (free plan at tavily.com). The key stays on this phone and is never put in backups.

YouTube search & audio download

Ember can search YouTube and download a video's audio to Music/Ember on your phone, with a progress notification. It only downloads when you ask, and only for links you gave it or that its own search returned. Only save things you have the right to keep. Needs the V1.6.0 APK.

Vision (read image links)

Ember can describe an image when you paste an https link to it. The link goes to Pollinations' free image reader, which fetches the picture. Ember cannot see photos you have not linked, and there is no camera or upload here.

Web Scraping
Enable Web Scraping & Saved Sites
Free reader · plain text, no key needed

The Free reader needs no key and is tried first: it fetches the page straight from this phone, then falls back to the free r.jina.ai and allorigins readers (those two see the link you read). Providers are then tried in this order, skipping any without a key: Firecrawl, Browserless, ScraperAPI, ScraperBee, Sting, ZenRows, SerpApi (search snippets only), Octoparse (not supported yet). Page text is sent to whichever chat engine answers, including Free Mode. Keys stay on this phone and are not in backups.

Local checks are instant. Provider checks fetch example.com once per provider, so each uses a small amount of that provider's credit.
